# Central Catalonia trees and shrubs (made in Blender, free to use, no credit needed)

Pick a plant by its **ID**. Look at `previews/OVERVIEW_ALL.png` (all trees at true scale next to a 1.75 m person), then open the `.glb` file in Blender (File, Import, glTF) or load it in your render scripts.

Models stand with the foot of the trunk at the origin, real metres, leaves are cut-out textures.

## Quick choice

- Shade and a solid dark green dome: **HO3** (large yard) or **HO2** (normal yard).
- Wild scrub look in a corner: **HO4**. Small plant near a wall: **HO1**.
- Typical hillside Mediterranean skyline: **AP2** or **AP3**. Artistic leaning tree: **AP4**. Young screen: **AP1**.
- Aromatic border or path edge: **RM1**, **RM2**. Spilling over a wall: **RM3**.
- Wild scrub, dark green wall: **KO2**, **KO3**. Low wild ground cover: **KO1**.
- Blue green accent: **JU1** (low), **JU2** (tall narrow), **JU3** (small tree).
- Yellow spring colour: **BR2** or **BR3**. Calm green stems: **BR1**.

They are stylised procedural plants (not scans), each variant is reproducible with `tools/make_catalan_trees.py`.
