Changes made: meshes merged to cut draw calls, textures resized and converted to WebP, geometry compressed
with meshopt. The characters are positioned and scaled in the scene. For the goblin, the sword was also removed. For the CV Tailor, textures were reduced and seven unused animations were removed.

All files were downloaded as GLB, checked for external references and required extensions (none), and
are treated as data only.
