## RESTRICTED: IKEA models (personal planning only, never ship)

The owner's render-master repository states these are IKEA product models supplied for personal planning, and must not be shared or made public. They are kept ONLY on the local disk in `assets-src/models/ikea-personal-use/`. That folder is listed in `.gitignore`, so Git never uploads it, and `scripts/build-assets.sh` never processes it, so nothing from it can reach `public/assets` or a deployed site.

Files: EKBACKEN worktop, FEJKA palm, IDANAS bed, JARNVAG rug, JATTEBO chaise longue, MATCHSPEL gaming chair, MITTZON desk, SIBBARP wall panel, SKALSTA table, TOCKNAN hob, UDDARP washer dryer, VINDSTOT fridge freezer, and a toilet.

Do not move any of them into `public/assets` unless IKEA's terms allow it for your purpose.
