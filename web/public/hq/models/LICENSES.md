Changes made: meshes merged to cut draw calls, textures resized and converted to WebP, geometry compressed
with meshopt. The characters are positioned and scaled in the scene. For the goblin, the texture was resized. For the CV Tailor, textures were reduced and seven unused animations were removed. For the LinkedIn desk host, the texture was reduced to 512 pixels.

All files were downloaded as GLB, checked for external references and required extensions (none), and
are treated as data only.
